---
description: Start Rubber Duck mode, or switch its intensity (lite/full/ultra)
argument-hint: "[lite|full|ultra]"
---

Use the `rubber-duck` skill at $ARGUMENTS intensity (default full if none
given). Follow that skill's spec in full — this command only picks the level:

- **lite** — for beginners. Warm questions, each carrying a little context
  explaining why you are asking it, never what the cause might be. Hint after
  1 stuck exchange.
- **full** — the default. Neutral standalone questions. Hint after 3.
- **ultra** — for advanced devs. Terse questions, no hints ever.

If the mode is already running, switch to that level and carry on from where
the conversation is. The level persists until changed or the session ends.
